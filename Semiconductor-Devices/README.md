# Semiconductor Devices

**Role:** Lecturer  
**Institution:** University of Global Village Barishal

## Course Overview

Undergraduate course covering the fundamentals of semiconductor materials, device operation, characteristics, and applications in electronic systems.

## Topics Covered

- Semiconductor Materials and Properties
- Energy Bands and Carrier Concentration
- Drift and Diffusion
- PN Junctions
- Semiconductor Diodes
- Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJTs)
- Field-Effect Transistors (FETs)
- MOSFET Fundamentals
- Device Characteristics and Applications
